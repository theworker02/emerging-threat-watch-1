RatHat

RatHat is an Android banking Trojan and remote-access backdoor that targets financial-account credentials, authentication data, and device access. It is distributed as sideloaded malicious Android applications through smishing, malvertising, phishing and deceptive download sites, and third-party forums. Victims are socially engineered into granting Android Accessibility Service permissions.

RatHat abuses Accessibility to automate activation of Developer Options and Wireless Debugging, read the local pairing code, and pair with the device-local Android Debug Bridge service. This gives its native components shell-level execution beyond the ordinary Android application sandbox. A Go-based agent executes commands, alters power-management settings, supports persistence, and can restore the primary application after removal. A reverse-proxy component establishes a persistent tunnel that gives operators remote access to the compromised device.

The malware steals credentials using overlays targeting banking, payment, and cryptocurrency applications; it also intercepts SMS messages and notifications to obtain one-time passwords and multi-factor authentication codes. RatHat captures typed text, browser address-bar contents, screenshots, installed-application inventories, files, and lock-screen secrets. Its shell-level component can record raw touch events and correlate them with device-specific keypad and pattern-lock layouts to reconstruct PINs and unlock patterns. RatHat serializes the Android Accessibility tree for generative-AI-assisted interface interpretation and adaptive on-device navigation.

RatHat incorporates anti-analysis and anti-debugging measures, including malformed APK container and manifest structures, DEX bytecode poisoning, encrypted strings, and checks for debugging, instrumentation, rooted devices, and emulators. Researchers have assessed, with limited confidence, that its operators may be China-based, principally based on Chinese-language artifacts; no specific threat group has been conclusively identified.
