## Analytical caveats

- Cruciferra ≠ Rapuncel author/operator — shared crypter/BYOVD tooling class only (DCRCVDrv ≠ Alinubx).
- Do not credit Cruciferra crypter capabilities to the Rapuncel stealer author without separate evidence.
- Cloudflare-fronted IPs have low specificity; prefer domains over CDN edge addresses.
- BoryptGrab is a lineage comparator only — same operator is not established.
- albinofennel.com NXDOMAIN (DoH 2026-09-19) is a DNS fact — not proof infrastructure is permanently gone.
- OBSERVED_PASSIVE lure/CT/DNS facts are not campaign-ownership claims.
