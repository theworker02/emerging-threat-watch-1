# Rapuncel — FBI / Field-Office Summary

**Package:** `ETW-RAP-IC3` · **Status:** DRAFT · **Cutoff:** 2026-09-19

## One-paragraph summary

I am reporting defensive threat-intelligence information concerning a malware distribution operation publicly documented as Rapuncel. LastPass TIME and Delphos published research on September 17, 2026 describing fraudulent, SEO-oriented GitHub repositories and GitHub Pages used to distribute a Windows information stealer. The reported chain used legitimate Microsoft vsdbg.exe for DLL sideloading and a malicious vsdbg.dll loader that the researchers assessed with high confidence as generated by Cruciferra/PUROSANGUE or a close derivative. Cruciferra is a crypter/delivery tooling context only; I am not equating Cruciferra authors or operators with the Rapuncel stealer author. The researchers published malware hashes, infrastructure domains and IP addresses. Passive follow-up (urlscan/CT/DoH) separately recorded an additional GitHub Pages lure host massimolongqdoj.github.io redirecting to albinofennel.com, certificate-transparency chronology for campaign domains, and NXDOMAIN for albinofennel.com as of September 19, 2026. eSentire Cruciferra IOC material (DCRCVDrv.sys / vsdbg sideload catalog) and the LOLDrivers CcProtect reference are retained as lineage comparison only. I have retained the original public research and indicator records. Independently validated infrastructure is labeled OBSERVED_PASSIVE and is not actor-ownership proof.
