## Paste-ready factual core (same as package narrative)

I am reporting defensive threat-intelligence information concerning a malware distribution operation publicly documented as Rapuncel. LastPass TIME and Delphos published research on September 17, 2026 describing fraudulent, SEO-oriented GitHub repositories and GitHub Pages used to distribute a Windows information stealer. The reported chain used legitimate Microsoft vsdbg.exe for DLL sideloading and a malicious vsdbg.dll loader that the researchers assessed with high confidence as generated by Cruciferra/PUROSANGUE or a close derivative. The researchers published malware hashes, infrastructure domains and IP addresses. I have retained the original public research and indicator records. Any independently validated infrastructure discovered during follow-up research will be separately identified as my own observation rather than attributed to the original researchers.
